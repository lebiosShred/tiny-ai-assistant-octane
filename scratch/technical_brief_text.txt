CUSTOMER’S GOAL 
The purpose of a sales system is to achieve the customer’s goal in the shortest possible time.  
Customer want to spend money and they want to buy your product. In their minds they have a goal the want to 
achieve, and time frame for which they want to achieve that goal. Our aim when building a sales system is not to make 
sales efficient for the company, but help customers achieve their goal in the shortest possible time.  
Often companies make the mistake of trying to be efficient at the expense of the customer's goal. Customers can 
sense when they are being herded. Example the introduction of self check-in at airports was an exercise in making 
operations efficient, not improving the customer experience. When self check in was first introduced it was clunky and 
required the traveller to key in many data points while holding passport and luggage.
