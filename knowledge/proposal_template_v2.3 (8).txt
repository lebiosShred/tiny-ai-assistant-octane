	Background 

The Client manages its entire budgeting and forecasting in Excel. There are approximately 5 spreadsheets which are brought forward into a single sheet. The Client is looking for an application to replace Excel, as they are experiencing several types of problems.

Version control is an issue. It is not easy to go backwards or forwards and witness the effects of a change. Further, Excel has limited access controls on who can see and who made changes.

Better budgeting and forecasting of costs. The second source of revenue – co-investment income requires better budget and forecast of costs, which are largely made up of payroll.

Allocations are inconsistent and time consuming. The organization has several business units - funds, corporate, investment bonds, and common investments. The finance team need to allocate expenses (particular payroll) to the various business units. The computations are duplicated and are often inconsistent.

Drill-through. From the report the Client must be able to see how an individual number was derived at the GL level.

Consolidating all the information. Currently the Client must manually post records from several Excel sheets into a single sheet.

They are assessing a few options including Workday and TM1. Anaplan did not make the final cut.

The primary use for TM1 is budgeting and forecasting. The Client has indicated they could work in several phases. Budgeting would be the first phase followed by forecasting as phase two. They are currently undergoing their budgeting process now – so first phase (budget) would ideally be a parallel run and complete by June 2022.

They also require dashboarding which could be done as phase three, as dashboarding is a nice-to-have.

“We need TM1 to move away from an excel environment, increase automation / efficiencies, reduce key man risk and work in a standardized budget/planning framework for the Group – headstock CNI level.” Greg Leung

 SAP integration 

TM1 has built-in features to facilitate integration of TM1 with SAP. Administrators can choose which views and tables to sync with TM1 from SAP, and what data to write back to SAP. Data sharing between the two applications is not real-time. Instead, data can be synced using a schedule or manually pushed with a single click.
