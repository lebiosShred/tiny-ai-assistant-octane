05 -- AUTOMATED REPORTS
For selected recorded calls, sales staff need to be able to generate structured reports from the transcript without manual writing. Claude is the intended tool for report generation. The sales person pastes the transcript (or relevant excerpts) into Claude and selects the appropriate report type.
Report types
1. Questionnaire answers. Transcript answers mapped to a predetermined question set. There are three versions depending on client type (First-time TM1 user, Existing TM1 user and First-time AI user)
2. Migration report. Based on the question and answers during the requirements session, create a branded migration report.
3. Recap email. A concise email summarising the three most salient points covered on the call. Intended for sending to the client immediately after the meeting.
4. Summary sheet. A brief, structured overview of what was discussed -- suitable for internal use or sharing with a colleague who needs to get up to speed quickly.
5. Notes. A more detailed account of the conversation, capturing context, nuance, and any points that don't fit neatly into a structured format.
6. Proposal. A drafted proposal generated from one or more call recordings. This draws on the accumulated context of the client relationship rather than a single call.
7. Action items. A clear list of who needs to do what following the call, with ownership assigned where identifiable from the transcript.
