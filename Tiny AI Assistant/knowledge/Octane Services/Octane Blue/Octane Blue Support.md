# Octane Blue Support & DevOps Playbook

Octane Blue is the most popular support tier provided by Octane Software Solutions. It operates on a flexible, pay-as-you-go model with no lock-in contracts, designed to support proactive systems development and incident management.

## Package Parameters
* Support Hours: 40 hours per month
* Priority Ticket Response: 1-hour SLA
* Standard Ticket Response: 4-hour SLA
* Project Work: Included (allows new enhancements and cubes under existing retainer)
* Advanced Reporting: Included
* Roll-Forward Hours: Included (unused hours automatically roll over to the next month)
* Free Training: Included (access to online training modules)
