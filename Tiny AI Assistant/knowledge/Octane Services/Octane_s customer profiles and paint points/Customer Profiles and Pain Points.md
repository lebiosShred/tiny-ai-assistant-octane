# Customer Profiles & Mapped Pain Points Playbook

This playbook outlines the nine key customer segments served by Octane Software Solutions, mapping their operational profiles, target decision-makers, core pain points, and current software tools.

## Target Customer Segments & Demographics

| Customer Segment | Target Personas | Typical Organization Profile | Product in Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large TM1 Shops | System Owner, IT Director | 200+ users. Internal support teams (often offshore). | TM1, Anaplan |
| Mid Size TM1 Shops | CFO, Head of FP&A | 20-100 users. $100M+ AUD revenue, 200+ staff (20 in finance). | TM1 |
| Small TM1 Shops | CFO, Head of FP&A | Under 20 users. Ad hoc support. | TM1 |
| TM1 Shops on Premise | CFO, Head of FP&A | running on-premise legacy models (Perspectives). | Perspectives, Excel |
| Supply Chain Prospects | CFO, Head of Supply Chain | Farms, Manufacturing, Retailers, Distributors. | TM1, Anaplan, Excel |
| New FP&A Prospects | CFO, Head of FP&A | Turnover $50M+ AUD. No current FP&A platform. | TM1, Excel, Anaplan |
| Enterprise AI (Large) | CFO, Finance Director | $500M+ AUD revenue. Finance team of 20+. 5+ days close cycle. | TM1, Excel, ERP |
| MidMarket AI | CFO | $100M-$500M AUD revenue. Finance team of 5-20. 5-10 days close cycle. | TM1, Excel, ERP |
| IBM PA + AI Upgrade | CFO, System Owner | Existing TM1 users. 50+ users. Active roadmap. | IBM Planning Analytics |

## Detailed Segment Pain Points

* Large TM1 Shops:
  * High cost of dedicated internal resources.
  * Difficulty accessing certified, high-quality TM1 developers.
  * Vendor lock-in with inflexible service contracts.
  * Low skill level of internal junior support resources.
  * Lack of support and development process maturity.

* Mid and Small TM1 Shops:
  * High cost of ad hoc vendor support agreements.
  * Project backlogs due to lack of developer bandwidth.
  * Ad hoc, reactive support without structured SLAs.
  * Internal IT drives roadmaps without business alignment.
  * Absence of ticket logging portals and professional desk operations.

* TM1 Shops on Premise:
  * Missing cloud benefits (uptime, scalability, auto-updates).
  * Outdated user interfaces (Excel Perspectives instead of PAW/PAfE).
  * High internal server maintenance and infrastructure costs.

* Supply Chain Model Prospects:
  * Manual inventory and demand planning calculations in Excel.
  * Disconnected supply and demand plans.
  * Inability to scale operational planning models.
  * Recurring data reconciliation errors.
  * High effort required to forecast and explain anomalies.

* New FP&A Prospects:
  * Manual budgeting and forecasting processes.
  * Inability to gain real-time business insights.
  * Lack of automated predictive/AI forecasting tools.

* Enterprise AI in Finance (Large):
  * Slow month-end close cycles (taking 5+ days).
  * Manual report creation consuming several business days.
  * Finance teams overwhelmed by repetitive internal queries.
  * Leadership demands AI adoption but lacks clear roadmap.
  * Zero visibility into the return on investment (ROI) of existing tech.

* MidMarket AI in Finance:
  * Manual and slow month-end reporting cycles.
  * CFO spends excessive time consolidating data rather than analyzing.
  * No self-serve reporting capability for external business units.
  * Drafting board packs takes too long.
  * Difficulty recruiting and retaining qualified finance staff.

* IBM Planning Analytics + AI Upgrade:
  * Legacy TM1 instances not delivering modern AI-powered insights.
  * Existing platform investment is under-utilized.
  * CFO asking "where is the AI layer?"
  * Reports remain manual despite major enterprise platform investment.
  * Peer pressure to demonstrate digital finance modernization.
