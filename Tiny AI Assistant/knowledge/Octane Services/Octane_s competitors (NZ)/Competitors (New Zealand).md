# IBM Planning Analytics Competitors - New Zealand

The New Zealand market is serviced by local branches of Australian partners as well as dedicated local specialists.

## Primary Competitors

* CorPlan New Zealand:
  * Status: IBM Platinum Partner (Auckland-based).
  * Specialty: Focuses exclusively on budgeting, forecasting, and business analytics. Actively hosts user groups and delivers model audits, health checks, and PAX migrations.
* Fusion5 New Zealand:
  * Status: Multi-regional Business Partner.
  * Specialty: Large-scale business applications partner. Implements IBM Planning Analytics to automate manual reporting cycles.
* Cubewise NZ:
  * Status: Global Specialist.
  * Specialty: High-end TM1 development, managed services, and Pulse-driven model management.
* Veracitiz NZ:
  * Status: Specialist Consultant.
  * Specialty: Focuses on financial workflow modeling and TM1 performance optimization.
