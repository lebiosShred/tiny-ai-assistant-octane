# Middle Game Sales Architecture

This document defines the middle game sales process, demo preparation guidelines, demo session execution protocols, the planning workshop pipeline, and the proposal negotiation/closing flow for Octane Software Solutions. Its purpose is to guide Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) and Solutions Architects in delivering high-impact product demonstrations, workshop sessions, and closing commercial agreements.

## 1. Pre-Demo Intake & Opportunity Registration

Before demo preparation begins, the sales representative must complete two foundational requirements:

* **IBM Deal Register**: Register the opportunity formally in the IBM partner portal to secure deal registration and support.
* **TM1 Snoop Video**: Review the prospect's legacy Cognos TM1 environment configuration (reconnaissance video/briefing) to gather structural insights.

---

## 2. Demo Preparation (30 Mins)

The architect and sales team dedicate 30 minutes to prepare the tailored environment.

### Key Activities:
* **Tech Stack Evaluation**: Review the prospect's source systems, database layers, and active reporting tools.
* **Feasibility Check**: Determine solution feasibility (verify "is the proposed model technically doable??").
* **Deliverable**: Draft the **Technical Validation on Solution** document outlining the architecture and modernization feasibility.

---

## 3. Meeting Confirmation & Execution

* **Meeting Reminder**: Dispatch an automated calendar reminder and confirmation email to the prospect.

### Demo Session Structure (1 Hour)
The demonstration is a structured 1-hour session split by pathway:
* **AI Demo Session**: For prospects interested in first-time AI planning integrations.
* **TM1 Demo Session**: For prospects focused on migrating or upgrading legacy Cognos TM1 systems.

### Timeline Breakdowns:
* **00:00 - 00:10 (10 mins)**: **TM1 Intro by Albert** (sets the stage, context, and goals).
* **00:10 - 00:40 (30 mins)**: **Core Demo** (demonstrates the specific solution highlights, speed, and usability).
* **00:40 - 01:00 (20 mins)**: **Q&A** (addresses technical questions and objections).

---

## 4. Post-Demo Follow-Up & Quality Review

* **Send Demo Video**: Follow up with the client within 2 hours, sharing the recorded demo session video for internal sharing.
* **Internal Call Review**: The sales and delivery team must review the demo call recording internally to evaluate representative performance, capture feedback, and refine the upcoming commercial proposal.

---

## 5. Collaborative Planning Workshop Pipeline

Prospects who complete the demo stage successfully are funneled into the collaborative workshop pipeline:

### Phase 1: Workshop Basics (30 Mins)
* **Objective**: Introduce and qualify the workshop value proposition.
* **Key SDR Activities**:
  * Sell the business value of the custom workshop.
  * Introduce the designated workshop leader/facilitator.
  * Capture basic client organizational information.
  * Book the formal collaborative session.

### Phase 2: Collaborative Workshop Session (1 Hour)
* **Format**: 1-hour interactive online workshop.
* **Structure & Timeline**:
  * **Introduction (10 mins)**: Session kick-off led by Albert.
  * **Interactive Model Building (30 mins)**: Build a live, collaborative visual model of the client's planning processes inside **Mural**.
  * **Data & Report Discovery (20 mins)**: Deep dive into the client's active reporting layout ("where is the data? Reports/Who is using it").
  * **Phased Roadmap Design**: Define a phased deployment approach to mitigate implementation risk.
  * **Pricing Bracket Review**: Present initial pricing ranges ("range price -- cheaper than you think") to establish budget alignment.

### Phase 3: Technical Checklist
* **Deliverable**: Technical qualification checklist completed by the solutions architect to verify integration points and requirements.

### Phase 4: Transformation Roadmap
* **Deliverable**: Modernization and transformation roadmap delivered to the client.
* **Follow-up**: Send the recorded session video to the client immediately after compilation.

---

## 6. Commercial Quoting & Licensing

* **IBM Quotation & Licensing**: Compile the official IBM Cognos/Planning Analytics licensing quote.
* **Pricing Calculator**: Utilize the commercial pricing calculator to determine subscription/licensing costs based on user tiers.

---

## 7. Proposal, Q&A, and Closing Pipeline

Once commercial quotes are finalized, the deal progresses through the formal review and closing pipeline:

### Phase 1: Proposal & Terms Generation
* **Deliverable**: Proposal and Terms & Conditions (T&Cs) package.
* **Follow-up Outreach**: Send a **Proposal Walkthrough Video** explaining the deliverables, timelines, and commercial models.

### Phase 2: Q&A Sessions
* **Large Clients**: Conduct a structured, formal Q&A session.
* **Medium/Small Clients**: Conduct a standard Q&A session.
* **Demonstrations**: Utilize the **Octane Blue Demo** or the **Octane Chatbot** interface to address specific client questions.

### Phase 3: Defer Questions & Revision
* **Defer Questions Email**: If technical questions cannot be answered live on the Q&A session, compile and send a defer questions email for offline research.
* **Update Proposal**: Refine the proposal based on Q&A feedback and resend the updated document.

### Phase 4: Optional Deep Dive Demo
* **Trigger**: Initiated for strategic prospects (specifically noted for clients like **Steric** and **NewCorp**) who require custom use cases before signing.
* **Objective**: Build a custom proof-of-concept (PoC) model to learn more about the prospect's unique operational challenges ("learning more about them ???").

### Phase 5: Proposal Signing
* **Objective**: Secure formal signature on the proposal to close the deal.
