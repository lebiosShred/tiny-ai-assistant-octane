# Competing Applications Playbook - Anaplan

This playbook documents the features, technology, and capabilities of Anaplan for Enterprise Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A).

## 1. Product Core & Technology
- Hyperblock Engine: Proprietary in-memory database technology enabling real-time, multi-dimensional calculations across massive datasets.
- Connected Planning (xP&A): Links finance plans with sales, HR, operations, and supply chain. Ensures a single source of truth.
- Scalable Hierarchies: Supports complex organizational structures (regional, product line, and corporate rollups).

## 2. Key Capabilities & AI Features
- Scenario & What-If Simulations: Users can immediately calculate the impact of changing drivers (e.g. price shifts, resource bottlenecks).
- Driver-Based Modeling: Key operational drivers automatically update dependent financial sheets.
- PlanIQ: Embeds machine learning algorithms to automate forecasting, select optimal statistical models, and decrease bias.
- Workflow & Integration: Integrates with ERPs via APIs (Anaplan Data Orchestrator) and supports task management notifications (via Slack or Teams).

## 3. Competitive Landscape
- High Scalability: Ideal for very large enterprise environments with massive datasets.
- Collaborative Ecosystem: Allows multiple departments to plan together in real-time, eliminating silos.
