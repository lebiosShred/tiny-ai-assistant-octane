# Early Game Sales Architecture

This document maps the early game sales process and SDR workflow for Octane Software Solutions. Its purpose is to guide Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) and enable the AI Assistant (Tiny) to assist them when they need guidance on shift handovers, call preparation, or customer positioning.

## 1. Shift Schedule & Booking Coverage

The Requirements Booking Page schedules coverage across two primary shifts based on Sydney (SYD) time:

* **Morning Shift (7:00 AM - 2:00 PM SYD)**
  * Primary SDR: Albert
* **Afternoon/Evening Shift (2:00 PM - 9:00 PM SYD)**
  * Primary SDR: Isha

---

## 2. Lead Generation & Pre-Call Preparation (Lead Sheet by Tiny)

When a lead books a slot, Tiny parses the intake details and generates the Sales Lead Sheet. The primary goals of this stage are:

* **Immediate Relevance**: Enable the SDR to establish credibility and relevance in the first moments of the call.
* **Preparation Advice**: Provide context on the prospect's industry, tech stack, and pain points before the call starts.

---

## 3. Requirements Session & Positioning Flow

During the call, the SDR conducts a requirements session with the goal of booking a Positioning Meeting.

### Action Checklist
* **Speed-to-Lead**: Call the customer within 30 minutes of booking.
* **Identify Competitors**: Probe for competing planning solutions (e.g., Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning, Board).

### Follow-up Protocol
* Send the **Requirement Email** summarizing the discussion.
* Send the **Requirement Invite** for the Positioning Meeting.
* **Optional Step (If no answer)**: Execute follow-up outreach only if the customer did not answer the phone.

---

## 4. Sales Pipeline & Meeting Handover Flow

The structured progression from the initial requirements session to the founder-level engagement follows a defined eight-step sequence:

### Phase 1: Requirements Recap Email
* **Deliverable**: Recap Email.
* **Content**: Highlights the top three salient points discussed during the initial requirements session.
* **Recipient**: Sent directly to the prospect immediately post-call.

### Phase 2: Branded Migration Assessment
* **Deliverable**: Migration Report.
* **Content**: Branded assessment detailing:
  * Current State (legacy systems, processes, tools).
  * Gaps Identified (manual workflows, performance bottlenecks).
  * Recommended Migration Path (cloud migration, platform upgrade, AI-layer addition).
  * Estimated Complexity (Low, Medium, or High with rationale).
  * Dependencies (blockers, prerequisites).

### Phase 3: Sneak Peek
* **Deliverable**: Interactive Preview.
* **Content**: Provides a high-level visual demonstration or teaser of the potential solution to maintain engagement.

### Phase 5: Meeting Reminder
* **Deliverable**: Calendar notification and confirmation email sent to the prospect.

### Phase 4: Handover Debrief
* **Deliverable**: Video Briefing.
* **Content**: A concise 2-minute summary video or brief.
* **Access**: Restricted to internal sales staff only to guide discovery in the next phase.

### Phase 6: Founder Coffee Session
* **Format**: 30-minute in-person coffee meeting.
* **Pathways**: Splits depending on client type:
  * **Founder Coffee Session TM1**: Tailored for clients using or migrating legacy IBM Cognos TM1 environments.
  * **Founder Coffee Session AI**: Tailored for first-time AI planning integration clients.
* **Key Session Activities**:
  * Deliver credentials to the prospect.
  * Position the **Octane Blue** modernization framework.
  * Listen actively to evaluate alignment and determine mutual fit (figure out if we are the right partner).

### Phase 7: Founder Debrief
* **Deliverable**: Post-session internal meeting to align next steps and deliverables.

### Phase 8: Share Octane Blue Video
* **Deliverable**: Branded presentation video walking the client through the custom-tailored Octane Blue architecture.
