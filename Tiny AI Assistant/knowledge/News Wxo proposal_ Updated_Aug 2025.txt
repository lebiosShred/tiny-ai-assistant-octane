	Background 

News Corp Australia operates a complex and fast-paced media business, requiring timely, accurate, and insightful financial reporting to support decision-making at all levels. The Commercial Finance team is responsible for producing monthly executive summary reports, financial insights, and ad hoc data analysis across the organization. However, these tasks were heavily manual, time-consuming, and prone to data inconsistencies, duplication, and rework.



The team identified several key challenges:



Significant time was spent on repetitive manual processes like data extraction, cleansing, and formatting.

Validation of data required extensive manual effort, especially when working across multiple systems such as TM1, Google Ad Manager, Adobe Analytics, and NewsQuery.

Limited self-service capabilities meant executives relied on analysts for basic queries, leading to delays and inefficiencies.

The growing volume and complexity of data made traditional reporting methods unsustainable.



Recognizing the potential of artificial intelligence to streamline operations, News Corp Australia engaged IBM Client Engineering and Octane to explore how Generative AI could modernize the financial reporting process. It was agreed that POC would be the best way to showcase how IBM AI technology could transform this function.



Approach: A Co-Creation Journey

Over five weeks, IBM, Octane and News Corp co-developed a tailored PoC focused on automating the generation of executive-level financial reports and enabling real-time data access through conversational interfaces.

The approach included:



Design Thinking Workshops: IBM and News Corp stakeholders collaboratively defined use cases, pain points, and user personas. The team selected two focus areas: automated report generation and conversational financial queries.

Data Integration: The team connected key enterprise systems (NewsQuery/BigQuery, TM1, Adobe Analytics, AdSlot) to create a unified data foundation. This allowed for consistent access to curated, validated financial data across reports.

AI-Driven Automation: Using Python and IBM’s watsonx Orchestrate platform, the team built an automation pipeline capable of generating structured PowerPoint slides containing charts, KPIs, and commentary. The pilot focused on automating five commonly used slides from the Monthly FNL (Finance News & Listings) report.

Conversational AI Layer: Leveraging IBM’s Bee AI framework, the team built a natural language query interface that allows users—particularly executive stakeholders—to ask financial questions and receive contextual, curated responses. This helped validate the vision of self-service analytics.

Security and Scalability Considerations: The solution was designed with role-based access, integration into News Corp’s cloud environment (GCP), and support for multiple concurrent users—ensuring it could scale securely to broader use.

Technical Demonstrations & Feedback Loops: Throughout the PoC, IBM and Octane demonstrated progress to the News Corp finance and technology teams, iterating on feedback and ensuring the prototypes aligned with user needs and business objectives.



Pilot Scope

Focused on five core slides from the executive financial report.

Included natural language financial querying via the “AskFinance” assistant.

Built as a modular, scalable framework with a clear pathway for future expansion.
