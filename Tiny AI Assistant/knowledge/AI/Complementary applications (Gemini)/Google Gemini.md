# Google Gemini Marketing & Sales Playbook

This playbook outlines the business capabilities, sales strategy, and enterprise value proposition of Google Gemini.

## 1. Core Value Proposition
* Native Multimodality: Trained from the ground up to understand, operate across, and mix different data formats including text, programming code, images, audio, and video streams.
* Massive Context Window: Supports up to 1 million tokens, allowing business users to process hours of video, audio recordings, or hundreds of pages of documents in a single analysis.
* Deep Google Workspace Integration: Embedded natively inside Gmail, Google Docs, Google Drive, Google Calendar, and Google Meet to summarize threads and draft assets.

## 2. Target Customer Profile
* Profile: Organizations heavily invested in the Google Workspace ecosystem who need advanced video/audio analysis, document summarization, and task orchestration.
* Buyer Personas: VP of Marketing, Head of Collaboration, Chief Digital Officer.

## 3. Marketing Value & Business Features (Mid-2026)
* Business Profile Sync: Links directly to Google Business Profile, allowing managers to analyze reviews, check search impressions, and draft responses from one screen.
* NotebookLM Research Agent: Secure cloud notebooks where users can analyze large datasets, perform complex math, build charts, and export directly to PowerPoint, Excel, or PDF formats.
* Daily Workspace Briefs: Automatically curates daily priorities, calendar timelines, and email thread summaries.

## 4. Enterprise Security (Sales Hook)
* Vertex AI Cloud Security: Provides enterprise-grade network isolation, Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK), data residency controls, and compliance certifications (HIPAA, FedRAMP High).
* Data Governance: Guarantees customer data is isolated and never used to train public Google foundation models.
