1. Deep Breakdown: The $0 Spend Tool Stack

🟧 HubSpot Professional
✅ Owned
Role Central System of Record & Routing Brain
Data I/O Native Syncs: 3CX (Audio), Fathom (Transcripts), Outlook (Calendar)

Exact Pipeline Utilization

  • Component 01 (Pre-Screen Calendar): Drives timezone-aware, shift-based routing (configured as Round-Robin in HubSpot to merge availability) with 12-hour booking buffers. Custom forms capture prospect context and automatically deploy Tickets into the "Early Game" sales pipeline via workflows.
  • Component 03 (Call Ingestion): Automatically ingests cold call audio metadata from 3CX and video transcripts from Fathom directly into the prospect's CRM timeline without rep intervention.
  • Component 04 (Call Directory): Serves as Anthony's definitive Coaching Hub. Enables chronological filtering across all reps, global transcript searching, one-click clipping, and the curation of Coaching Playlists natively inside the CRM.

HubSpot Deal Board Stages

  1. Lead Captured: Initial contact details and lead capture.
  2. MQL: Marketing Qualified Lead status verified.
  3. Prescreen Booked: Pre-screen call scheduled.
  4. Prescreen Completed: Pre-screen call completed.
  5. Discovery Booked: Positional meeting/deep dive scheduled.
  6. Discovery Completed: Discovery findings documented.
  7. Positioning Meeting: Presentation of proposed services/solutions.
🤖 Claude AI (Anthropic)
✅ Covered
Role Cognitive Processing & Synthesis Engine
Data I/O Manual (Copy/Paste Workflow via 2 Dedicated Projects)

Exact Pipeline Utilization

  • Component 02 (Pre-Screen Prep): Reps use the "Tiny - Pre-Screen Prep" Claude Project (powered by the Tiny AI Sales Assistant and loaded with OneDrive catalogs). Ingests LinkedIn PDFs and HubSpot form data to instantly output a structured 10-point briefing with 3 specific conversation starters.
  • Component 05 (Automated Reports): Manual Copy-Paste via Claude Projects. Reps manually copy and paste transcripts from Fathom/Jamie AI into the "Tiny - Sales Reports" Claude Project (powered by Tiny) to generate 6 structured artifacts (Questionnaire Mapping, Recap Email, Summary Sheet, Detailed Notes, Proposal Draft, and Action Items) to build qualifying muscle memory, copy-pasting them back into HubSpot. (Phase 2 Upgrade: Zero-touch automation via Zapier/Make.com + Claude API).
📞 3CX
✅ Owned
Role Outbound Dialing & Cold Call Audio Capture
Data I/O Native API Sync to HubSpot (SupportsTranscription="true")

Exact Pipeline Utilization

  • Component 03 (Cold Calls): Executes all outbound dialing. Automatically records the call and pushes the audio URL and call metadata straight to the prospect's HubSpot timeline. Raw audio (WAV) is available for downstream transcription routing if native parsing fails.
👁️ Fathom AI (with Jamie AI Fallback)
$0 / mo (Free Tier)
Role Video Transcription & Telemetry Ingestion
Data I/O MS Teams (Bot Joiner) → HubSpot (Native Sync) | Local Client (Jamie AI)

Exact Pipeline Utilization

  • Component 03 (Video Calls): The Fathom bot silently auto-joins all scheduled MS Teams video meetings. It captures the entire recording and generates highly accurate verbatim transcripts within 2-5 minutes of call termination.
  • GDPR Fallback (Jamie AI): If prospects push back on the Fathom bot, reps deploy Jamie AI locally. It runs invisibly on the rep's device, with data hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Component 04 (Sync): Pushes Fathom recording link/transcript directly to HubSpot. (Jamie AI transcripts are uploaded manually).
Ⓜ️ MS Teams & OneDrive
✅ Owned
Role Video Hosting & Base Knowledge Storage
Data I/O Teams → Fathom | OneDrive → Tiny (Manual)

Exact Pipeline Utilization

  • Teams (Component 01 & 03): Acts as the underlying VOIP carrier for all scheduled video meetings booked via the HubSpot calendar, providing the raw audio/video payload that Fathom requires for transcription.
  • OneDrive (Component 02): Functions as the definitive static repository for Octane's service catalogs, product sheets, and past job specifications. Reps download this baseline data and feed it into the Tiny AI assistant to maintain contextual accuracy.

Component 01

Pre-Screen Calendar

Prospects land on a branded Octane pre-screen page setting clear expectations. They book a time via a shift-based combined calendar link (Albert & Isha, configured as Round-Robin in HubSpot) or direct calendar links (Amendra & Steny), preventing same-day bookings and ensuring the rep has time to prepare.
System 🟧 HubSpot CMS & Calendar
Component 02

Pre-Screen Preparation

Before the call, the rep gathers 6 manual inputs (including LinkedIn profile, company email, and intake answers) and feeds them into the Tiny - Pre-Screen Prep Claude Project. The Tiny AI Sales Assistant instantly returns a structured 10-point briefing with likely objections and 3 conversation starters.
System 🤖 Tiny AI Assistant (Claude Prep)
Component 03

The Call & Transcription

The rep runs the call focusing entirely on the prospect, without taking notes. They follow the 12-question qualification script (aligned with Octane's services). The conversation is recorded and transcribed in the background.
System 📞 3CX (Cold) / Fathom (Video)
Component 04

Central Call Directory (Coaching)

Transcripts and audio flow directly into HubSpot's native Calls index. Anthony logs into one system to review chronological call feeds, search transcripts, and clip moments into Coaching Playlists for the team.
System 🟧 HubSpot Calls Index
Component 05

Sales Reports (Manual-First)

Reps manually copy and paste transcripts from Fathom/Jamie AI into the "Tiny - Sales Reports" Claude Project to generate 6 structured artifacts (Questionnaire Mapping, Recap Email, Summary Sheet, Detailed Notes, Proposal Draft, and Action Items) via the Tiny AI Sales Assistant, then record a 2-minute Vidyard screencast. (Phase 2 Upgrade: Webhooks trigger Zapier/Make.com + Claude API to automate the pipeline).
System 🤖 Tiny AI Assistant (Claude Reports) / ⚡ Zapier + Claude API (Phase 2) + 🎥 Vidyard
