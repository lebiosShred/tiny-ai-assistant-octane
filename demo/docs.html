Introduction & Product Architecture

The Tiny AI Assistant is an enterprise-grade sales pipeline coprocessor designed specifically for Octane Software Solutions to optimize prospect intake, pre-screening discovery preparations, and call synthesis deliverables. It aligns with standard playbook rules to support designated Sales Representatives — specifically Albert and Isha — in executing discovery operations while enforcing strict pricing sovereignty guidelines.

Business Objectives & Team Roles

The coprocessor implements a disciplined, simple sales process to generate and qualify high-value leads for Octane Software Solutions, resolving the lack of structure in early pipeline stages that historically caused deals to stall. The system design and workflow coordination are distributed across key stakeholder roles:

  • Albert & Isha: Designated Sales Representatives responsible for conducting pre-screen discovery calls and pipeline advancement.

The Mega Prompt Architecture

The "Mega Prompt" is a highly structured, operational instruction set designed to lock the AI into a deterministic operational state. It is injected into the Claude Project configuration to eliminate hallucination and enforce rigid enterprise compliance.

The Mega Prompt is constructed upon 5 architectural pillars:

  • [ROLE]: Defines the exact persona the AI must emulate (e.g., "You are an elite Pre-Sales Engineer at Octane"). This anchors the tone and bounds the AI's domain knowledge.
  • [CONTEXT]: Dynamically passes in the prospect's background, LinkedIn profile, and historical data to ensure the AI's logic is perfectly tailored to the current deal.
  • [TASK]: The strict sequence of analytical instructions the AI must execute (e.g., "Analyze the inputs to build a 12-section technical briefing dossier").
  • [GUARDRAILS]: Hard negative constraints ensuring system safety and data sovereignty (e.g., "If context is missing, explicitly flag [NEEDS RESEARCH]. Do not hallucinate metrics. Do not invent integrations").
  • [OUTPUT FORMAT]: Forces the AI to return data in a parseable UI structure (e.g., exact Markdown headers, limited bullet points, strict JSON structures), ensuring it maps perfectly into the sales team's frontend application.

Structural Architecture of the "Mega Prompt" Template

The AI Assistant relies on a complex, highly-structured "Mega Prompt" framework rather than standard conversational queries. This approach front-loads all context, constraints, and output formatting in one shot to eliminate back-and-forth iterations and prevent hallucination. The template enforces strict control over the LLM through the following structural blocks:

  • ROLE: Defines the exact persona and domain expertise. By instructing the model to act as a "Senior B2B Sales Strategist specializing in IBM Planning Analytics", the LLM adopts industry-specific vernacular, priorities, and analytical frameworks instead of responding like a generic chatbot.
  • CONTEXT: Injects the aggregated ground-truth data. This section strictly bounds the AI's knowledge base to the prospect intake data, extracted LinkedIn profiles, GDrive SOW attachments, and scraped company history.
  • TASK: Provides the primary directive. This outlines the exact operational objective -- for example, "Generate a 12-section briefing dossier covering competitor mapping, likely pain points, and three strategic conversation starters."
  • GUARDRAILS: Implements safety and sovereignty constraints. Guardrails explicitly define negative parameters, such as "Do not invent statistics. If insufficient data exists, flag the section as [NEEDS RESEARCH]. Never offer discounts or guarantee implementation timelines." This eliminates unauthorized commitments and hallucinated claims.
  • OUTPUT FORMAT: Dictates the structural presentation. The prompt enforces formatting rules (e.g., Markdown headers, strict bullet limits, no introductory filler) to ensure the generated output directly maps to the application's UI components and remains highly scannable for the sales team.

Manual Research Augmentation

When the AI flags sections as [NEEDS RESEARCH] -- for example, when a prospect's LinkedIn is private or the booking form answers are vague -- the sales representative can manually augment the inputs:

  1. Open the Tiny AI Chatbot Widget: Click the chat bubble (Watsonx Orchestrate) in the bottom-right corner of the console.
  2. Ask Targeted Research Questions: For example, "What does Meridian Logistics sell and who are their main competitors?" or "Find the most recent press release from Meridian Logistics."
  3. Copy & Paste Findings: Copy the chatbot's response and paste it into the relevant Input Text form field on the preparation panel.
  4. Re-run the Dossier: Submit the enriched context to generate a more complete briefing with fewer gaps.
Human-in-the-Loop The AI accelerates research -- it does not replace strategic thinking. The representative always reviews the output and adds their own domain judgment before the call.

Component 04: Live Discovery Call

Step 2 contains call facilitation tools, including teleprompters, discovery scripts, and pre-compiled icebreaker dossiers. The requirements session lasts exactly 30 minutes and is facilitated by Albert or Isha.

🎬 Module 4 Walkthrough: Executing the Discovery Call (Albert/Isha) ⏱️ 1:01

Meeting Objectives & Sales Team Rules

Representatives must adhere to the 5 primary objectives during the pre-screen call:

  1. Build Rapport: Open the call using the 3 conversation starters compiled in the Rapport Guide (Component 03).
  2. Execute Discovery Questionnaire: Walk the prospect through the 12 structured questions from the battlecard script.
  3. No Typing Allowed: The representative must focus fully on conversation. Answers are captured via automatic transcription (Fathom or Jamie AI).
  4. Post-Call Synthesis: The representative copies the transcript from the recording tool and pastes it into Step 3 to map answers and auto-generate reports.
  5. Schedule Hand-over: Before ending the call, the representative must book a Positional Meeting with Amendra.

Recording Contingency & Upload

The primary recording tool (Fathom AI) automatically joins virtual meetings via calendar integration. However, bot-based recording can fail due to waiting room settings, IT restrictions, or invalid meeting links. The sales team follows a structured contingency plan:

  1. Plan A -- Automatic Recording: Fathom AI auto-joins and records the session. Transcription is immediate.
  2. Plan B -- Platform Native Recording: If Fathom fails to join, activate Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams built-in recording as a backup.
  3. Plan C -- External Recording Tools: For phone-based discovery calls, use 3CX (PBX call recording, WAV format) or FreJun (cloud dialer with automatic recording and AI transcription).
  4. Plan D -- Manual Capture: If no recording is possible, capture the 4 Pillars: Pain Points, Impact, Decision Process, and Next Steps. Apply the 10-Minute Post-Call Rule: brain dump, structure, and confirm via recap email.

After the call, the representative uploads the recording file (MP3, MP4, M4A, or WAV) via the Upload Recording button in the Session tab. The system automatically transcribes the audio and populates the transcript field for synthesis.

Pre-Screen Call Rapport Guides

Asynchronously generated upon Dossier compilation, the Rapport Guide accordion auto-expands on step transition. It summarizes findings into actionable call prompts:

  • Opening Statements: Curated hooks to build immediate rapport.
  • Key Facts: Vital prospect metrics (budget parameters, consolidated sheet counts).
  • Core Pain Points: Primary business obstacles identified during booking.
  • Forbidden Phrases: Guardrail warnings blocking representatives from mentioning unapproved discounts or service items.

Lead Classification & Sales Funnel Stage

In addition to the six deliverables, the synthesis engine automatically classifies the lead based on transcript analysis:

Classification Definition Transcript Signals
🔥 Hot Ready to buy immediately Pricing inquiry, defined timeline, decision-maker engaged, comparison language
🟡 Warm Interested but not yet ready Educational questions, exploring options, no defined timeline
🧊 Cold Early stage or unknown Vague questions, no prior engagement, no budget indication

Recommended Next Action Based on the classification, the system recommends the appropriate follow-up: immediate proposal for Hot leads, nurturing sequence for Warm leads, or re-engagement campaign for Cold leads.

Component 06: Meeting Booking

Securing the next engagement step is critical for pipeline progression. The final stage of the coprocessor workflow involves generating and sending the Positional Meeting invite. This bridges the gap between technical discovery and executive decision-making.

The Positional Meeting Architecture

The Positional Meeting is a highly structured, 45-minute executive briefing led by Amendra or Steny. It represents the official hand-over from the Sales Representative to the Executive Director. The objective is to leverage the technical pain points uncovered during the discovery call to validate the prospect's business case and align it with Octane's strategic offerings.

  • Executive Validation: Ensures that the prospect's challenges (e.g., consolidation lag, broken Excel formulas) are reframed into business-level risks (e.g., regulatory compliance failures, delayed board reporting).
  • Pricing & Roadmapping: Executive Directors introduce the tiered proposal and establish implementation timelines.
  • Stakeholder Expansion: The meeting mandate requires the prospect to invite their internal budget approvers, transitioning the deal from end-users to decision-makers.

The Booking Playbook Variant

To secure this meeting without causing friction, representatives utilize the Positional Transition Playbook. This playbook ensures the prospect understands the value of the follow-up meeting rather than viewing it as a generic sales pitch:

  1. The Pivot: "Based on the complexities you described with your current Anaplan integration, I need to bring in our Executive Director to map out a specific architectural solution."
  2. The Value Exchange: Offer a tangible asset (e.g., a custom demo, a gap analysis report, or a reference architecture diagram) that will be presented during the Positional Meeting.
  3. The Assumption Close: Rather than asking for a meeting, prescribe the next step. "I will go ahead and put a 45-minute placeholder on our calendars for next Tuesday to review the proposed architecture with Amendra."
ICS Generation & CRM Sync Upon completing the synthesis workflow, the coprocessor generates a pre-configured .ics calendar file containing the synthesized summary. The representative downloads and sends this directly to the prospect, bypassing manual calendar entry while ensuring the Executive Director is fully briefed.

Customer Profiles Playbook

Understanding the prospect's background is essential for tailoring the pitch. This playbook defines the three primary buyer personas encountered during discovery calls, outlining their core metrics, operational challenges, and common objections.

Persona 1: The Tactical Finance Manager (The "Excel Warrior")

  • Profile: Typically a Finance Manager or FP&A Lead managing a chaotic web of interconnected Excel spreadsheets. They are drowning in manual reconciliation.
  • Key Metrics: Time-to-close, error rates, hours spent validating data.
  • Primary Pain Points: Version control nightmares ("Which file is the final version?"), broken macros, system crashes during peak reporting periods, and the inability to perform rapid scenario modeling.
  • Common Objections: "We don't have the budget for a massive implementation," or "Our team is too busy to learn a new system right now."
  • Playbook Counter: Focus on immediate ROI. Emphasize how Planning Analytics automates data ingestion and eliminates manual checks. Highlight that familiar Excel interfaces (PAfE) ensure a low learning curve for their team.

Persona 2: The Disillusioned System Owner (The "Legacy Migrator")

  • Profile: A Director of Finance or IT Systems Manager who inherited an underperforming or rigidly implemented system (e.g., Anaplan, Jedox, or legacy TM1).
  • Key Metrics: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), model calculation speeds, IT ticket resolution times.
  • Primary Pain Points: The current system is too slow, too expensive, or lacks the flexibility required to adapt to new business models. They are frustrated by external consultancy fees required for minor adjustments.
  • Common Objections: "We just spent a fortune implementing this system three years ago," or "Migrating away will disrupt our entire operation."
  • Playbook Counter: Attack the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). Demonstrate how Octane's rapid deployment and superior TM1 multi-dimensional engine drastically reduce calculation times and maintenance overhead, paying for the migration within 18 months.

Persona 3: The Strategic Executive (The "Growth Driver")

  • Profile: CFO or VP of Finance looking to scale the business, prepare for an acquisition, or deploy advanced analytics (AI/ML).
  • Key Metrics: Revenue growth, EBITDA, forecasting accuracy, strategic agility.
  • Primary Pain Points: Lack of real-time visibility into the business. The inability to forecast cash flow dynamically. They are frustrated that their team is acting as "data historians" rather than strategic advisors.
  • Common Objections: "Is this just another dashboarding tool?" or "Can this handle our future M&A strategy?"
  • Playbook Counter: Pivot from features to strategic capabilities. Emphasize continuous, integrated planning and AI-driven forecasting. Show how Watsonx and TM1 can model complex future scenarios instantly, enabling data-driven board-level decisions.
